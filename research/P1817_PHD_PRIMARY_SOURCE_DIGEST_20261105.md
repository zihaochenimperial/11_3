# Coordinator primary-source checks for Claude follow-up

Date2026-11-05. These are coordinator observations of actual web-tool primary records. They do not mean Claude independently read every full text. All are literature observations, not reproduced scientific results.

- https://arxiv.org/html/2602.09415v1 Feng/Kao2026: full HTML opened. Abstract/introduction describe nonlinear block-structured inverse problems, GS forward operator, local identifiability assumptions, stability-concentration bounds, stability-resolution tradeoff. It addresses broad operator-level limits rather than a noise-aware adaptive parameter-direction algorithm. No judgment here of theorem correctness. Requires reading assumptions rather than treating title as decisive.
- https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05522v1 Jia et al2026: full HTML opened. Bayesian3DGS NIW geometry posterior using renderer-derived surrogate summaries; optional DP complexity signals; posterior rerender predictive uncertainty and interval calibration. Explicit approximate-vs-closed-form inference boundary. Sparse/fixed-budget view-acquisition experiments. Therefore generic Bayesian GS + uncertainty + adaptive complexity is already occupied. Different imaging observation and decision can matter, but3D-to2D migration alone is weak novelty.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10219 Hanson et al2024 preprint/CVPR2025: primary abstract and CVF paper excerpts opened. PUP sensitivity pruning uses second-order reconstruction error approximation w.r.t. per-Gaussian spatial parameters and prune-refine. Therefore Fisher/Hessian pruning not new.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17074 and https://repo-sam.inria.fr/fungraph/reduced_3dgs/reduced_3DGS_i3d.pdf : Papantonakis et al2024 adaptive per-Gaussian SH coefficient count, resolution-aware pruning and quantization. Generic adaptive degrees of freedom not new. Shape-direction unlocking under a known degradation operator is more specific but unproven novel.
- https://github.com/JiangWenPL/FisherRF primary README opened: active view selection and radiance-field uncertainty via Fisher information;2023 preprint and2024 conference records both found by Rabbit. Therefore Fisher-based GS information/uncertainty not new.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01866 Image-GS primary abstract opened: adaptive2D image Gaussian allocation, progressive fitting, error-guided hierarchy, joint compression/restoration. Generic2D Gaussian restoration not new.
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19108 GaussianImage++ and https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17896 LocoADC primary records opened; exact algorithm-level overlaps still need full method comparison.

Coordinator cautions on first Claude analysis: per-Gaussian diagonal Fisher can falsely signal identifiability when neighboring primitives compensate. Raw parameter eigenvalues depend on units/parameterization and label/splitting symmetries; need projected reconstruction effects, nuisance-adjusted blocks or comparison invariant to reparameterization. High Fisher curvature alone does not imply useful model expansion; approximation bias and predicted improvement also matter. Identical primitive colors/locations supply counterexamples to 'color almost always identifiable'. CRB is a bound under assumptions, not promised equality. Blur can correlate heldout signals; independence must be formulated in measurement space. Conformal pixels not generically exchangeable. All pass thresholds proposed by Claude are prospective suggestions, not existing accepted contracts.

Classical alternatives independently located via web primary sources: Tibshirani *Degrees of Freedom and Model Search*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.1920 (2014preprint,2015publication; search degrees of freedom and discontinuous Stein extension). Ramani/Blu/Unser *Monte-Carlo SURE*,2008, https://www.ee.cuhk.edu.hk/~tblu/monsite/pdfs/ramani0803.pdf (black-box perturbation risk estimation for iterative denoisers, tested on TV and other algorithms). Therefore 'search costs complexity' and 'apply MC-SURE to a black box' are themselves established. A prospective Gaussian method needs additional methodological/theoretical value or a substantial new empirical conclusion. Targeted searches for Gaussian splatting plus search degrees of freedom/Stein returned no directly equivalent method in this bounded pass; absence of a returned result is not proof of absence.
